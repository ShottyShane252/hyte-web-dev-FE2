About Dear Diary

Our Mission

Dear Diary was created to help people track their health journey in a simple, private, and meaningful way. We believe that small daily habits lead to big changes in overall wellness.

How It Works

Each day, you can log your mood, sleep quality, water intake, exercise, and notes about how you're feeling. Over time, you'll see patterns that help you understand what works best for your health.
